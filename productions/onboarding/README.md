# onboarding

Customer explainer for Nautt company onboarding. 16:9, EN / PT-BR / LatAm ES.
Macro level: what is required, why, and the timeline — from the user’s side and from Nautt’s.

## Story (6 scenes)

| # | Scene | Message |
|---|-------|---------|
| 0 | Hook | Short verification before you operate; protects you, partners, and the platform. |
| 1 | Map | Four parts: information → documents → liveness → wait for approval. |
| 2 | Info & docs | What you provide + why Nautt needs it (real business & people before money moves). |
| 3 | Liveness | Short selfie + why (not opened with stolen documents alone). |
| 4 | Timeline | Submit → Nautt reviews → partners verify → email → dashboard unlocks; wallet in background. |
| 5 | Close | Clear steps. Secure platform. Welcome to Nautt. |

## Guardrails

- No field-by-field UI walkthrough (no “trade name / annual volume / Carpeta…” laundry lists).
- Never brand SAIQ, Veriff, SumSub, or Lumx.
- Nautt is a technology platform; keep the film operational, not legalistic.
- Partners who were added also verify their own identity (one beat on the timeline).
